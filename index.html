  • 1,000genes mapped in stromal cells
  • 6networks: LH+3 to LH+11, plus RIF
  • 10gene territories, each with a hub regulator

What it shows

How transcription factors switch genes on and off in the uterine lining, and how that wiring changes across the cycle and in recurrent implantation failure.

How it was built

Single-cell RNA-seq from 28 women, networks inferred per cell type and timepoint with CellOracle, cross-checked with dynGENIE3.

What it adds

Every gene carries its implantation literature from NCBI and PubMed, so known regulators and novel candidates can be told apart at a glance.

Links in the atlas are computational predictions, not proven cause and effect. demo_v1 is for review, not for citation.
