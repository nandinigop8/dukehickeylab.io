    Glossary
    RIF
    Recurrent implantation failure
    LH+7
    Window of implantation (7 days after LH surge)
    Regulator (TF)
    Transcription factor: switches genes on/off
    Importance
    How central a gene is (eigenvector centrality)
    Link strength
    Model estimate: + on, − off
    Known link
    Also reported in experiments (CollecTRI)
    Confirmed by 2 methods
    Key regulator in CellOracle and dynGENIE3
    Novel candidate
    Central here, little research: a lead
    Limits of this map
    • Links are predictions from expression, not experiments.
    • One biopsy per woman; RIF sampled only at LH+7 and pooled.
    • Territories are an algorithmic grouping, not proven pathways.
    • Literature is collected automatically; "Draft" notes await lab review.
