STUDY DESIGN

One tissue, five cell types, six moments to compare

Fertile subjects were each biopsied once, at one of five timepoints relative to the LH hormone surge. RIF (recurrent implantation failure) subjects were biopsied only at LH+7 — the window of implantation — so they can be compared directly against fertile cycles at that same day.

INTERACTIVE EXPLORER

Explore the regulatory network

Scrub across the fertile timeline to watch the network rewire, or switch to Fertile vs. RIF to see exactly which connections are gained, lost, or retained at the window of implantation. Node size reflects that gene's centrality in the current network; click any gene for detail.

Fertile
Someone who gets pregnant without difficulty. These are the "normal" cycles this study uses as the baseline.
WOI (window of implantation)
The few days each cycle — around day 7 after ovulation (LH+7) — when the uterus lining is actually ready to accept an embryo. Before or after this window, implantation can't succeed even with a healthy embryo.
RIF (recurrent implantation failure)
Patients whose embryos have repeatedly failed to implant despite multiple attempts. All RIF samples here were taken at LH+7, the same day as the fertile WOI, so the two groups can be compared fairly.
"WOI trajectory" mode
Scrubs through a normal fertile cycle (LH+3 → LH+11) to show how the network rewires over time.
"Fertile vs. RIF" mode
Freezes on day LH+7 and compares fertile subjects to RIF subjects at that same day, highlighting what's different.

TWO INDEPENDENT METHODS

Why every network here is cross-checked

Agreement between two structurally different inference methods is treated as real evidence — disagreement is reported, not hidden.

CellOracle

Discrete, per-group · what the Explorer draws above
  • Starts from a genome-derived motif prior — which TFs could plausibly bind which gene's promoter
  • Fits one bagged ridge-regression model per group (6 groups), using only that group's cells
  • Pruned to the top 2,000 edges per group (p < 0.001)
  • Directed, signed edges — coefficients can be activating or repressing
  • The only method that includes RIF

dynGENIE3

Continuous, pseudotime-based · fertile only
  • No base-GRN constraint — considers every TF against every modeled target gene
  • Orders fertile cells along an estimated pseudotime, binned into 150 bins
  • Random-forest regression (500 trees/target) predicts expression across the trajectory
  • One continuous importance score per regulator→target, not tied to a single day
  • No RIF model; no model at all for T cell (pseudotime failed validation, ρ = 0.23)
a gene flagged by both methods is labeled dual-validated
KEY FINDINGS BY COMPARTMENT

What the networks show

Confidence badges reflect how well each compartment's pseudotime recovered real chronological order — a caveat carried over directly from the methods documentation, not a stylistic flourish.

Stromal Strong pseudotime

Densest, most TF-driven network in every condition. RIF adds new edges converging on TCF7L2/EBF1 while the RORA–ZEB1 core stays wired — an addition on top of the normal program, not a breakdown of it.

Epithelial Weaker pseudotime

Sparser, hub-centered. Driver genes lean structural/ciliary (CFAP126, DYNLRB2, KIF9), often shifting individually rather than as a wired cluster. RIF rewires around the same RFX3 hub.

uNK / ActivatedNK Moderate pseudotime

Two established, distinct tissue-resident immune programs — deliberately not merged into one "NK" bucket. See each compartment's ranked-regulator tables for dual-validated drivers.

T cell No dynGENIE3 model

Pseudotime failed the pre-registered validation gate (ρ = 0.23, threshold 0.30). Excluded by design — T cell genes can never be "dual-validated" and should never read as a false negative.

CellOracle only

Not causal validation Read carefully

Every edge combines a sequence-based motif prior with a statistical association — no chromatin or perturbation data confirms mechanism. Treat edges as plausible relationships, not proof.

Study-design confound Read carefully

Fertile subjects are destructively sampled, one timepoint per person — the WOI "trajectory" compares different people, not the same person over time.

LIMITATIONS (click to expand)
Not causal validation ⌄
Every edge combines a sequence-based motif prior with a statistical association across cells in this group. No chromatin-accessibility or perturbation data confirms the relationship is causal — treat every edge as a plausible regulatory relationship supported by this analysis, not a proven mechanism.
RIF was only sampled at LH+7 ⌄
There is no RIF trajectory across the cycle, and no dynGENIE3/pseudotime model exists for RIF at all. Any Fertile-vs-RIF comparison in this data is valid only at LH+7.
Pseudotime reliability varies sharply by compartment ⌄
Stromal's pseudotime is the strongest in the analysis; Epithelial's is distinctly weaker; uNK and ActivatedNK are weaker still; T cell failed the validation gate entirely and has no dynGENIE3 model. dynGENIE3-based claims should read with correspondingly less confidence outside Stromal.
Compartments aren't comparable to each other ⌄
Cell counts differ up to ~4x between compartments, which alone produces different network sizes independent of any real biological difference. Compare a compartment's network across time/condition — never one compartment's node count against another's.
RIF-vs-fertile edge diffs are pooled, not per-subject ⌄
Gained/lost/retained calls are computed from pooled RIF-LH+7 vs. pooled Fertile-LH+7 networks — not yet verified to be consistent across subjects individually. Avoid wording that implies a specific edge is a confirmed, cohort-wide effect.
